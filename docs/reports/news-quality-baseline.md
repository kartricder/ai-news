## Dataset Limitations

All URLs use .invalid and all events/model names are fictional scenarios, not verified announcements. Source-kind labels are stipulated, not measured trust; historical source names are synthesized from title or source kind. Shared synthetic padding provides length/vocabulary but is not real independent article evidence. Case order is grouped and not a random representative sample. In particular all five Vietnamese holdout cases are negatives (Vietnamese important recall is undefined), event holdout emphasizes security/business and research holdout is narrow. Pair holdout has only seven examples and cannot establish general cluster precision. No statistical confidence or production accuracy claim is justified. Keep this v1 fixture frozen; a future human-labelled, stratified, sourced v2 needs a fresh untouched holdout and separate comparison.
